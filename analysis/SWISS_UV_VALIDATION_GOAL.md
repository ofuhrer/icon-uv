# Broader Swiss UV validation goal

Defined 6 September 2026. Validate the unchanged icon-uv methods and daily map
outputs against as many actual Swiss UV measurement sites as can be discovered
and accessed. Use the authorized Balfrin DWH client, supplemented by primary
archives where useful. Preserve all earlier campaigns and their conclusions.

Start with the four SACRaM sites (Davos, Jungfraujoch, Locarno-Monti, Payerne)
and PMOD/WRC measurements at Davos and Weissfluhjoch; search DWH metadata and
other primary inventories for further usable sites. Distinguish instruments,
co-located streams and geographically distinct sites. An ozone instrument,
modelled UV product or broadband solar pyranometer is not automatically a UV
observation suitable for validation.

Inventory parameter identity, erythemal weighting, units, timestamp/averaging
support, calibration/QC status, site coordinates/heights, period, access route
and missingness. Small availability probes may count records without inspecting
UV outcome values. Prefer corrected final radiation streams and their own QC
flags; raw voltages and uncalibrated signals must not be converted by a guessed
constant. Preserve the distinction between DWH plausibility and SACRaM QC.

Use the existing fixed 150-date native ICON campaign (August 2024–August 2026),
both forecast days and complete daylight windows wherever the observation
archive permits. Add bounded recent complete cycles for current-configuration
evidence where useful. Keep the Payerne reservation of 84 dates intact and
freeze its accepted adapter before computing errors. Do not tune the candidate
from newly observed errors. All observational exclusions stay in the roster.

Deliver site/season/snow/forecast-day scores for raw UVI, displayed integers and
categories, paired baseline/shortwave attribution where supported, dependence-
aware intervals and explicit small-sample limitations. Independently verify
unit/time conversions, daily aggregation and summaries. Retain source/input/
code identities and reproducible acquisition and replay commands in a new work
directory. Finish with a coverage table or map and an updated readiness decision.
An inaccessible or unqualified series is documented, not counted as validated.

No fitting, scientific outreach, public data release or recurring job is implied.
